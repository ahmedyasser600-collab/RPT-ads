## Fact basis (checked 4 Oct 2026)
UAE Ministry of Finance **Ministerial Decisions No. 243 and 244 of 2025**, with the large-business provider
deadline moved by **Ministerial Decision No. 66 of 2026**:

| Group | Appoint an Accredited Service Provider | Go live |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | n/a | 1 Jul 2026 |
| Revenue ≥ AED 50M | 30 Oct 2026 (was 31 Jul 2026) | 1 Jan 2027 |
| Revenue < AED 50M | **31 Mar 2027** | **1 Jul 2027** |
| Government entities | 31 Mar 2027 | 1 Oct 2027 |

Scope: B2B and B2G invoices, exchanged as structured e-invoices (PINT AE) through accredited service providers,
with data reported to the Federal Tax Authority.
Sources: [Ministry of Finance announcement](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-the-issuance-of-two-ministerial-decisions-on-the-scope-of-obligations-and-the-timelines-for-implementing-the-electronic-invoicing-system-2/) ·
[KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/10/uae-framework-scope-implementation-e-invoicing-system.html) ·
[VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/07/18/uae-two-ministerial-decisions-set-e-invoicing-scope-and-timeline/) ·
[ClearTax](https://www.cleartax.com/ae/e-invoicing-uae) ·
[Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/blog/en/europe/2026/03/uae-e-invoicing-mandate-2026-readiness-asp-pint-ae.html).

### Guardrails
- The video **does not claim TOLX is an accredited service provider**. It also doesn't claim any Odoo version has a certified UAE
  e-invoicing connector, or offer tax advice. The offer is getting sales, VAT and invoicing into one system, ready to connect
  to the provider the business appoints. Change this only once TOLX confirms what it delivers (for example, a named ASP partnership).
- The flow diagram is labelled "Simplified" (the real model is a multi-corner exchange). TRN and amounts are placeholders.
- The audience is framed as "selling to other businesses", so consumer-only kiosks aren't told the mandate applies to them.
- The footnote cites the decisions and says "Not tax advice". The chip reads "Illustrative workflow".
- The voice is synthetic (Higgsfield TTS V2, ElevenLabs, preset **Gia**), with a 1.06× read and presence EQ/compression (kit `ENERGY_FX`).
