## Fact basis (checked 4 Oct 2026)
Odoo 20 was released on 24 September 2026 at Odoo Experience. The three features come from Odoo's own announcement
and release notes:
- **AI agent:** "automate any process by telling your AI agent what to do in plain language… it explains exactly what
  it's about to do before the process runs". Odoo's own examples include "alert on low stock".
- **Accounting AI agent:** ask about receivables, gross profit or cash flow and get figures from your reports.
- **Point of Sale offline mode.**

Sources: [Meet Odoo 20 (Odoo blog)](https://www.odoo.com/blog/odoo-news-5/meet-odoo-20-2439) ·
[Odoo 20 release notes](https://www.odoo.com/odoo-20-release-notes) ·
partner summaries [Aktiv Software](https://www.aktivsoftware.com/odoo-20-features-and-updates/),
[Hadron](https://hadron-for-business.odoo.com/blog/aktualnosci-2/odoo-20-is-here-what-s-new-and-what-it-means-for-your-company-105).
odoo.com itself was blocked from this build environment, so the quotes come from Odoo's search excerpts. Re-check
the release notes before posting if wording matters.

### Guardrails
- The screens are TOLX-style recreations, **not Odoo screenshots**, under an "Illustrative workflow" chip with the footnote
  "Illustrative screens and figures. Not a client project." Amounts, customers and orders are invented.
- "Odoo 20" is set in TOLX type; Odoo's logo isn't recreated. The **Odoo Ready Partner badge** is the official file from the
  TOLX theme (`assets/odoo_ready_partners_rgb.png`), unchanged apart from proportional resizing, on a white card for legibility.
  You confirmed TOLX may use it.
- **Odoo AI features consume paid credits (in-app purchase)**, so nothing in the video says AI is free. Mention it in the caption.
- No prices, savings, upgrade times or UAE e-invoicing claims. E-invoicing is planned as video 04.
- The narration never says "TOLX" (pronunciation not yet confirmed).
- The voice is synthetic: Higgsfield TTS V2, ElevenLabs engine, preset **Gia**. Takes are in `audio/vo_lines/g*.mp3`, with the old Marcus takes in `m*`.
  For energy, the script uses short exclamatory lines, the read is sped up 1.06× (pitch kept) with a presence EQ and compression (`ENERGY_FX` in the kit),
  and the score runs a 124 BPM groove from the first frame with bass hits on each title slam.
